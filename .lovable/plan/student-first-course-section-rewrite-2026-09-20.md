# Student-First Course Section Rewrite

## Goal
Rewrite the existing course section so a student can quickly understand what they will learn, how the learning works, what “job-ready” means, and why the course is more useful than disconnected free tutorials.

## Changes
- Replace agency-style headings and sales language with direct student questions and answers.
- Keep the four existing course names, audiences, durations, filters, comparison disclosure, and enquiry behavior.
- Give each course a short, concrete learning list:
  - Tech Ready Kids: explore tech roles, build and explain a practical project, and learn how skills connect to work.
  - Ready Before Your Batch: match a resume to a job description, practise STAR interview answers, introduce yourself clearly, and target suitable roles.
  - Resume & Profile Optimization: align resume language with role requirements, strengthen achievement bullets, and improve profile positioning.
  - Recruitment Certification: read job requirements, source and assess candidates, structure interviews, and use AI without replacing human judgment.
- Show the known duration on each course and describe the commitment honestly as 30–60 minutes per learning session. Do not invent a duration for Tech Ready Kids.
- State the shared delivery format clearly: live sessions, recorded lessons, practical assignments, and mentor feedback.
- Add a concise “Why this is different from free tutorials” explanation focused on structured order, real job examples, personal feedback, and accountability.
- Define “job-ready” as being able to tailor a truthful resume, answer behavioural questions with a framework, and target suitable roles deliberately.
- Remove unsupported callback percentages, placement statistics, guarantees, platform claims, and vague promises such as “premium opportunities” or “earn first income.”
- Keep one consistent action: “Find Your Best Fit.”

## Validation
- Check filters, course expansion, comparison disclosure, and enquiry selection.
- Confirm the section is readable on mobile and desktop with no browser errors.
